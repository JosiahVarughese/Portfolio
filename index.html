My History With Programming

I started programming back in 2009 when I was about 14 years old.

At the time I was really into 3D modeling & animation. I was thrilled one day when discovered the 3D renderer I had been using had a built in game engine.

I immediately started using this to build many of my own games, I worked with this engine up until 2012 when I discovered Unity3D Unity, a free realtime 3D engine intended for game development, used actual code, C# specifically. Though timid at first, I eventually worked up the nerve to teach myself how to write code and quickly fell in love with it. There were many weeks during my highschool years that I would wake up at 5:00am, go straight to the computer and code until 6:00pm, only stopping to eat. I worked on increasingly ambitions and complex projects, feeling the thrill of my confidence growing every time I conquered a problem that initially seemed beyond me.

During this time I touched subjects like physics, advanced vector math, procedural generation, and even neural networks. I crashed my computer more times than I care to admit, but I was learning a lot and having a great time!

Over the years I eventually started focusing on building simulations rather than games, these simulations were meant to answer questions, fascinated with what code and curious about what I could achieve with it. I was really starting to see and understand the connection between math, logic and the natural world around me, programming was a tool that could be used to express any idea and explore any subject.

In 2019 I started taking some online courses for web development, during this time I experimented with building moderation chat bots for the discord server I was in. I would often put in wacky features to entertain my friends. At one point I actually turned the whole chat into a text based video game. It was shortly after this that I applied to a software engineering internship under York Solutions and began my career in web development.
